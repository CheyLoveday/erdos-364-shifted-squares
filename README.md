# Paper I companion repository

This repository is the private, clean-history construction workspace for the
Paper I publication surface on shifted-square obstructions at indices divisible
by 17 or 41.

## Current authority and release state

This is a private Tranche 0 bootstrap. It is not a public release, submission,
licence decision, canonical-status record, or Ledger admission. No public
visibility, arXiv submission, JNT submission, DOI deposit, release tag, or
claim expansion is authorised here.

## What is and is not proved here

The bootstrap itself proves no mathematical statement. No human proof, Lean
declaration, finite certificate, replay, theorem sheet, or claim-ceiling
statement has been promoted into this repository yet.

The intended Paper I scope concerns fixed 17/41 shifted-square work. Whole
Erdos 364 closure and unrelated candidate-prime research are outside this
bootstrap.

## Authority model

The private LAB remains the research workbench and provenance authority for
frozen upstream objects. This repository is the sole editable publication
workspace for the future article and supplement. A manuscript file must not be
edited independently in both places.

Each future promotion must be one-way, committed, required by a named
publication consumer, and recorded before use in
evidence/PROMOTION_LEDGER.md.

## Trust surface

Human proof, Lean verification, exact computation, independent replay,
regression, and provenance are distinct forms of evidence. Their roles are
defined in TRUST.md. None is established by the existence of this bootstrap.
