# Authority and one-writer rule

## Roles

- LAB is the private research workbench and provenance authority for frozen
  upstream objects.
- This companion is the private publication-source authority for the Paper I
  article, supplement, and curated publication evidence.

## One-writer rule

After bootstrap, an article or supplement file may have one editable
publication authority: this repository. LAB material may be selectively
promoted one way, but there is no automatic or bidirectional manuscript sync.

## Construction boundary

Only committed, identifiable source objects may be considered for promotion.
Promotion does not authorise claim expansion, public visibility, submission,
licensing, canonical-status change, or Ledger movement. Those decisions remain
with Chey.
