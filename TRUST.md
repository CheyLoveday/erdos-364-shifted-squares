# Trust boundary

| Evidence form | What it can establish | What it does not establish by itself |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Human proof | The stated mathematical argument under its recorded hypotheses | Formal elaboration, finite data, or release status |
| Lean verification | The exact checked declaration under its imports and recorded axioms | The human exposition, finite certificates, or broader prose claims |
| Exact computation | A deterministic result for its declared finite input | An unbounded theorem or a separate implementation |
| Independent replay | Agreement for the declared replay contract | A broader claim than the replay contract |
| Regression | Continued detection of a covered mutation or defect | Mathematical completeness |
| Provenance | Identity and lineage of an object | Validity, rights clearance, or publication authority |

Tranche 0 contains no promoted mathematical evidence.
